## 🛡️ STEP-BY-STEP CASHOUT ACTION PROTOCOL (ZERO TAX & ZERO HASSLE)

1. **Do NOT withdraw directly to local crypto wallets or P2P:**  
   P2P crypto cashouts violate FERA 1947 and AML Act 2012. Always use the institutional wire path.
2. **Payout Pipeline:**  
   `Prop Firm Dashboard` ──► `Offshore Neobank (nsave / EU EMI in Fiat USD/EUR)` ──► `SWIFT Wire` ──► `Bangladeshi AD Bank`.
3. **Automatic Clearance below $20,000:**  
   Under Bangladesh Bank FEPD-1 Circular No. 26, remittances up to $20,000 are credited automatically without manual Form-C submission.
4. **Form-C Submission for $20,000+:**  
   When single payouts exceed $20,000, attach this filled Form-C with the prop firm payout invoice/dashboard screenshot and submit to your bank manager.
5. **100% Tax Exemption:**  
   Under Income Tax Act 2023 (Sixth Schedule, Part 1, Para 21, modified 2026-27), all remittances under Code 2410 (ITES/BPO) are **100% Tax-Exempt and VAT-Free**.
6. **ERQ Account Facility:**  
   Open an Exporter's Retention Quota (ERQ) account with your bank to retain up to 50% of your earnings in pure USD to legally pay for VPS, servers, and quant tools without dollar endorsement limits.
