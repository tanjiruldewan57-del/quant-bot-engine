## 📝 2. Step-by-Step Registration Protocol (Bangladeshi National)

### Step 1: Account Type Selection (CRITICAL)
* Go to [Payoneer.com](https://www.payoneer.com) and click **Sign Up / Register**.
* When asked *"I am a..."*, select: **Freelancer or SMB**.
* When asked *"I am looking to..."*, select: **Get paid by international clients and marketplaces**.
* When asked for monthly volume, select: **Between $1,000 and $10,000**.
* On the next page, select account type: **Individual** (do NOT select 'Company' since you don't need a trade license yet).

### Step 2: Personal Identification
* Fill in your full name **exactly as printed on your National ID (NID) / Smart Card**.
* Provide your active email and date of birth.
* Enter your permanent Bangladeshi residential address.

### Step 3: Security & 2FA
* Choose a strong password and save the security question answers securely.
* Add your Bangladeshi mobile number and verify via OTP.

### Step 4: Link Your Local Bangladeshi Bank Account
* Payoneer requires one existing local bank account for withdrawals:
  * Select Country: **Bangladesh**.
  * Currency: **BDT**.
  * Choose your bank (e.g., Dutch-Bangla Bank, BRAC Bank, City Bank, Islami Bank, Mutual Trust Bank, etc.).
  * Enter Branch Name, Account Name, and Account Number.

## 💸 5. How to Bring Money into Bangladesh Bank
Once you see the USD balance in your Payoneer app and feel 100% confident:
1. Click **Withdraw $\to$ To Bank Account**.
2. Select your linked Bangladeshi bank (e.g., DBBL, City Bank, BRAC Bank) or bKash.
3. Enter the amount (e.g., $1,500 USD).
4. Payoneer converts to BDT at competitive central bank exchange rates and deposits directly via National Payment Switch Bangladesh (NPSB) or BEFTN within 24 to 48 hours.
5. You receive an SMS from your local bank confirming foreign remittance received.
